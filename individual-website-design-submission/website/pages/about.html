Our story

Good care makes room for good living.

Evergreen & Gleam was imagined around one simple idea: caring for your home should feel helpful, human, and dependable.

Our purpose

Care that looks beyond the checklist.

We bring housekeeping and landscaping together because the spaces we live in do not stop at the front door. A refreshed kitchen, a tidy living room, a healthy lawn, and a cared-for garden all contribute to a place that is easier to enjoy.

Our approach is practical and people-first. We listen, agree priorities, communicate clearly, and treat each property with the same respect we would want for our own.

What guides our work

  • Respect: your time, belongings, privacy, and preferences matter.
  • Consistency: agreed checklists and clear communication help us keep standards steady.
  • Care: we aim to leave every space feeling noticeably better cared for.
  • Responsibility: we choose sensible products and garden practices suited to the task.
