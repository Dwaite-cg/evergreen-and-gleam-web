How we handle enquiries.

This is a student portfolio website for a fictional company. Below is a plain-language notice about its contact form.

Information collected

When published on Netlify, the contact form collects the details that visitors choose to submit, such as their name, email address, phone number and enquiry.

Use and retention

Submitted information is used only for responding to the enquiry in this demonstration project. The site owner must review Netlify's form submission storage, access settings and retention before deploying. This site does not implement advertising or analytics tracking.

Fictional business information

Evergreen & Gleam is a fictional educational project. Its listed prices, phone number and email address are examples. Do not send sensitive personal information using this form.

Contact

For questions about the assignment website, contact its developer through the GitHub repository once it has been published.
