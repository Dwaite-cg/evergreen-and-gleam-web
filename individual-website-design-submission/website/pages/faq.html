Frequently asked questions

Start with the basics.

Can I book housekeeping and landscaping together?

Yes. You can request both in one enquiry. We’ll confirm what can be combined in a single visit and what may need a separate appointment.

Are the prices on the website final?

No. They are illustrative starting rates for this sample website. A real quote should be confirmed after discussing the space, condition, location, access, and your requested checklist.

Do I need to provide cleaning or garden supplies?

Supply arrangements should be confirmed when booking. Please mention any product preferences, sensitivities, or tools already available at the property.

Can I ask for recurring visits?

Yes. Weekly, fortnightly, monthly, and seasonal schedules can be discussed depending on the type of service and availability.

What should I do before the team arrives?

Secure valuables and pets as needed, share access instructions, identify priority areas, and point out delicate surfaces or plants that need special attention.

What if I need to reschedule?

Contact the service team as soon as possible. A real provider should share its cancellation and rescheduling policy clearly when the booking is confirmed.

Do you offer commercial or rental-property care?

Small workplaces, rental properties, and move-related requests can be discussed. Availability depends on the job scope and the service plan.

How do I get an accurate quote?

Use the contact page to describe the property, services, preferred timing, and any special details. A final estimate can be provided after the scope is clarified.
